A student can do well in Class 10 and still feel lost in Class 11 Physics. They follow worked examples and learn formulas, but an unfamiliar question leaves them unsure where to begin. As difficulties build, confidence can fall. Additional tuition can add study hours without resolving the confusion.

We developed icanlearnit’s **one-month Physics bridge course** to help students make this transition with understanding. We are a US-based ed-tech startup inviting educators to help shape the course.

Class 11 Physics introduces differentiation and integration in *Motion in a Straight Line*, followed by vectors in *Motion in a Plane*. Mathematics develops these tools across Classes 11 and 12, through chapters including *Limits and Derivatives*, *Integrals* and *Vectors*. Students are trying to understand the Physics while still getting to know the mathematics.

Our course brings them together through physical questions. Measurements become graphs. Slopes explain velocity. Changing velocity introduces acceleration and differentiation. Adding small changes builds towards integration. Trigonometry and vectors extend that understanding to oscillations and motion in two dimensions.

Worked examples, guided questions and independent practice help students build the reasoning themselves. Diagnostic assessments identify gaps, while the supporting platform organizes coursework and helps teachers follow progress.

Understanding also gives students a reason to care.
