## Prosody and cognitive load

The emotional movement remains **confusion → connection → curiosity → confidence**. Each section develops the same message. The page establishes the course; the invitation remains subtle.

The opening gives the reader the audience, offer and collective expertise. The founder does not lead. The course cover is a typographic presentation of the offer, not a screenshot or reconstructed logo.

The capstone diagram begins with different-looking questions and reveals their common reasoning below. It is a conceptual dependency view, not a chronological lesson schedule. The lower layers use actual course concepts, not an invented branded pedagogy. The rate-of-change definition comes directly from the course: “Rate of change tells us how one quantity changes with respect to another.”

Keep the main explanation visible without clicking. Do not replace the approved capstone visual with slope/robot demonstrations, a two-ball animation, or a generic before/after metaphor. Full equations, course outline and practical pilot details can sit in disclosures.

Use direct, positive professional language. Benefits are aims. Individual affiliations describe participating professionals; they do not imply institutional sponsorship. Specific delivery commitments and expert research examples require current supplied evidence.
